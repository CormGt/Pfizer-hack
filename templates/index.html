🔒 Privacy check: your knowledge stays yours

Live from the app. Everything below is worked out right now, not a promise written in advance.

Check it yourself, without trusting this app

  1. Turn off Wi-Fi and use the app. It still works, because nothing needs the internet.
  2. While the app is running, in Terminal run lsof -nP -iTCP | grep -i python. Every line should show 127.0.0.1 or localhost (this computer), nothing else.
  3. Look in the app's folder: there's no database or notes file. Your notes are only in your own folders.
  4. Stop the app (Ctrl + C). Anything it held in memory is gone.
  5. The whole app is one file, app.py. Search it for socket.socket.connect to see the network lock.
